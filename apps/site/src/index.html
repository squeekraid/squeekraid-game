How it works

Squeektoshi runs on his wheel at home. Squeekchain records each run. SqueekRaid turns that record into rivers.

1

A wheel run is recorded

Squeekchain measures the wheel day by day, from noon to noon UTC.

2

The run becomes a river

Each half hour of running shapes a stretch of river. Every Daily route is built from a recorded day, the same river for everyone.

3

You fly it

Steer, shoot the cats, collect seeds to keep your fuel from running out, and go as far as you can through five worlds.

Genesis is fixed forever: Squeektoshi's first recorded night, the all-time challenge. A new Daily arrives every day at 00:00 UTC.

Questions

The game

What is SqueekRaid?

A free pixel arcade game you play in your browser. Fly a small plane down a river, shoot the cats, collect seeds to keep your fuel up, and see how far you get. The rivers are drawn from the recorded wheel runs of Squeektoshi, a real hamster on Squeekchain.

Is it free?

Yes. There is nothing to install and nothing to pay. Signing in for online play is a wallet signature, not a transaction: it costs no gas and moves no funds.

What are Genesis and the Daily?

Genesis is one fixed river, built from Squeektoshi’s first recorded night: the same for everyone, forever, with an all-time leaderboard. The Daily is a new river every day at 00:00 UTC, built from his most recent recorded day (noon to noon UTC). If a day has no usable recording, the last good route is used again and labelled with the day it really comes from.

How do I fly?

Keyboard: ← → or A and D to steer, Space or J to fire, Z, X and C (or 1, 2 and 3) for Gear, P or Esc to pause. You can change every key in Settings. On a phone, drag on the river to steer and hold FIRE with another finger.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern mobile browser. Phones and browsers vary, so tell us if something looks wrong.

Is the hamster activity in the game live?

Routes come from Squeektoshi’s recorded runs and say so. Live Drops, when online play opens, appear only while the official Squeekchain data shows him running at that moment. Nothing in SqueekRaid simulates his activity.

Online play and accounts

How do I join online play?

Online play is in a closed beta. Connect your wallet, link X, and enter your invite code in the game. Watch @SqueekRaid on X for news about the beta.

Why connect a wallet and X?

Your wallet is your SqueekRaid account: it keeps your Gear, collection and results. Linking X puts your name on the leaderboards next to your verified flights.

Is my wallet safe?

SqueekRaid only asks you to sign a sign-in message. It never asks for your seed phrase or private key, and never asks you to approve a transaction or a token allowance. If anything claiming to be SqueekRaid asks for one, it is not us.

Why isn’t my score on the leaderboard?

Only online flights that the server has verified are listed, and only for pilots with both a wallet and X linked. Practice results never appear on the boards.

How do I sign out?

Click the Wallet or X button at the top of this page and choose Disconnect or Sign out. Both end your session on this browser.

Gear and rewards

What are Mystery Boxes?

On the online Daily, Mystery Boxes appear along the river. Each box you collect opens in the Hangar into one piece of Gear.

What does each Gear do?

Bubble Shield absorbs one hit. Spread Shot fires three ways for a while. Seed Tank tops up your seeds. Long Barrel gives your shots longer range for a while.

How do I use Gear in Genesis?

Before an online Genesis flight, pick a loadout of up to 3 Gear types, up to 2 of each. Fire them during the flight with Z, X and C. A piece of Gear is used up when you activate it.

When do boxes and Gear expire?

At the next 00:00 UTC after the Daily flight that earned them, opened or not. Use them the same day.

What are Live Drops?

Rare cosmetic capsules that can appear only while the official Squeekchain data shows Squeektoshi running at that moment. They change how things look, never how the game plays.

Is anything for sale? Is there a token?

At the moment nothing in SqueekRaid is for sale. Gear and cosmetics are in-game items, not tokens or NFTs.
