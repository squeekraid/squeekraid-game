Earlier days
Lab

Rules v3: fixed speed, and pressure that keeps rising with distance with no last stage. Cats learn new tricks as you go: tracker drones, pincer pairs, mine layers, strafing jets with an edge warning. Seed depots are never more than 338 m apart. The wheel data only shapes the river.

How to play

←→ steerSpace fireZXC GearP pause

Shoot the cats, collect seeds so your fuel never runs out, and fly as far as you can. Every flight can pause for 20 seconds in total.

The routes

Genesis is fixed forever: Squeektoshi’s first recorded night, the all-time challenge. A new Daily is built every day at 00:00 UTC from one of his recorded wheel runs: busy nights make wild rivers, sleepy nights make calm ones.

Practice and Online

Practice runs entirely in your browser with a practice Gear kit; results stay on this device. Online flights are replayed by the server and only a flight that reproduces exactly counts for the boards and rewards.
